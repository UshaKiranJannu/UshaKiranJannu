### About Me

I'm a Data Engineer with 5+ years of experience designing and building scalable, cloud-native data pipelines on **Azure** and **AWS**. I work across the full data lifecycle — ingesting raw data from on-prem and streaming sources, transforming it at scale with Spark, and delivering it into warehouses like **Snowflake**, **Synapse**, and **Redshift** for analytics and reporting.

I care most about pipelines that are **correct, observable, and trustworthy** — not just ones that run. Recent work has focused on real-time streaming with exactly-once semantics, schema evolution with Iceberg, and cutting pipeline latency and manual ops overhead through better orchestration.
